# Skills

Skills are reusable procedures.

Here they are portable skill package/templates. Do not claim they auto-load unless the local Codex environment proves that behavior.

Mistakes prevented:

- inconsistent handoffs
- broad claims without proof
- scope drift during implementation

Exercise: use the proof-mapper skill to downgrade an overbroad success claim.

Client offer angle: create client-specific skill packs for support, QA, release, and incident response.
