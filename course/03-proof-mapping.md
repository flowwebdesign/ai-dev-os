# Proof Mapping

Proof mapping turns a claim into evidence requirements.

A UI claim may need action, API/network, persisted state, reload/readback, and user-visible proof. A unit test alone may be useful but still partial.

Mistakes prevented:

- "works on my machine" releases
- screenshots treated as complete proof
- green tests hiding broken hosted behavior

Exercise: take one feature claim and label the available evidence as verified, partial, smoke, not run, or blocked.

Client offer angle: define proof matrices for client release workflows.
